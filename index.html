Do I need a degree to apply?

No. Passing 12th is the minimum. Skills matter more to us than certificates.

Can freshers apply?

Yes. We train freshers who are curious and ready to learn on real client work.

What should my resume include?

Your education, any projects or samples you have made, and the tools you use. One page is plenty.

How soon will I hear back?

Shortlisted candidates get a call or email from our team. Please keep your phone number and email correct.
